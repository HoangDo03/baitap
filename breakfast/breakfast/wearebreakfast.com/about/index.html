Breakfast is remixing the
ingredients of the
trailer music world.

Breakfast is a creative music studio that reshapes sound into stories. We are a band of sonic ninjas that are boldly breaking down audio cliches.  We’re driven by one single guiding principle: to bring the most cutting, connected, memorable sound to everything we touch. Our sounds are built to make people stand up and say “Whatever that was… I WANT MORE OF IT!!!”

Founded in Los Angeles by composer / producer Raney Shockne and music talent manger Kevin Held, the idea was hatched to use their 20+ years of success to reshape the trailer music business and create sonic art that will stun and amaze.
